decision: redesign
summary: The digest is a copy that gets added to at four separate trigger points (root epic creation, nested append, add-only re-entry, a one-line no-beads rule). Make the specs' Key abstractions sections the only source, and regenerate the epic digest, owner-bead entries, no-beads plans and designer input from all committed sections whenever any section changes. That removes 4 of the 6 confirmed findings.
