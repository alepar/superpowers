The rebuild runs in the task worktree after the clean check (the shared `stackStep` helper runs "still in ${worktree}"); detaching frees the branch for `rebase --onto`; a stack-merge conflict never moves the branch ref; `rebase --abort` restores the ref. Implementation choreography, not a spec gap.
- §4.4 no migration/cutover for in-flight runs — Rejected 2/3 (ground seat Nit). Historical gaps are accurate reports of real omissions; §2 requires omissions be visible; exit 3 retains partial results (super-auto/SKILL.md ~L240); resume compatibility is not a stated requirement.
